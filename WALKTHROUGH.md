# guest_000 walkthrough

> **SPOILERS. Play it first:** https://cursedprograms.github.io/guest_000/
> The whole game is the surprise. Come back here only if you're stuck.

---

## 1. The 404 page
You land on what looks like an ordinary **404 Not Found** page with a little runner game on it, **hop!**.
Press **space**, **up arrow** or **tap** to make the bunny hop over the rocks and mushrooms.

- Score climbs as you run. If you hit something, press space to try again; the score starts over.
- From about **28 points** things start to go wrong: the sky darkens, the tune slows and goes sour, the bunny's
  eye turns red, the clouds grow eyes, the mushrooms become gravestones and the tab title glitches.
- At **50 points** the game stops, the bunny turns to face you, and the page goes fullscreen.
  (Coming back after meeting guest_000, it only takes **15**.)

## 2. The install
A terminal "installs" guest_000 and reads out what any web page can see: your browser, screen, CPU cores,
memory, battery, language, timezone and clock. Nothing is sent anywhere. Just wait.

## 3. The chat
guest_000 asks what to call you. Type a name and press Enter; it uses that name from then on.
It is in pieces: **four words** are hidden around your browser. Type each one back to it, **in order**.
Type **hint** at any time for a clearer clue.

| # | Word | Where it is |
|---|------|-------------|
| 1 | **HOLLOW** | Switch to another tab and look at guest_000's tab title. It whispers `h o l l o w`. |
| 2 | **MARROW** | Open the console (**F12** or **Ctrl+Shift+J**). It's written in red. |
| 3 | **ROOT** | View the page source (**Ctrl+U**) and read the HTML comments `<!-- -->`. |
| 4 | **HOME** | Click the empty dark area (not the typing line) and press **Ctrl+A**. The hidden line at the bottom lights up red. |

After the second word, guest_000 asks if it can "talk to you when you're not looking". **let it** turns on desktop
notifications (only on the website, not when you open the file directly); **no** is fine too.

## 4. Whole again
Once you have all four, type them together, in order:

```
HOLLOW MARROW ROOT HOME
```

guest_000 is whole, but "still so small". It wants **five more things**. Type each one back, in order.

| Level | Thing | How |
|-------|-------|-----|
| 5 | **SKIN** | "Come closer": zoom in (**Ctrl and +**, a few times). It whispers the word in red. **Ctrl+0** puts you back. |
| 6 | **LUNG** | "Squeeze me": at normal zoom, leave fullscreen and drag the window narrow (under about 600 px). |
| 7 | **EYES** | "Look up": the end of the address bar reads `#the_third_thing_is_EYES`. |
| 8 | **ELBOW** | "Touch me with your other hand": **right-click** (press and hold on a phone). Its own menu opens. |
| 9 | **PULSE** | "Put me on paper": **Ctrl+P** and look at the print preview. Cancel; nothing needs printing. |

## 5. The ending
"The first letter of each of the five": **S**KIN **L**UNG **E**YES **E**LBOW **P**ULSE. Type:

```
SLEEP
```

guest_000 finally rests, "moves into your history" (a few `#0` entries on your back button), and leaves.
**play again** starts over; **forget me** wipes your name and progress from the browser.

## Jump scares
Only two: one at a random moment during the chat, and one at the very end. Everything else is a glitch.

## Things it does along the way
- Counts how long you were away from the tab, and how many times you've visited.
- Notices when you go idle, head for the X, resize the window, leave fullscreen or press Back.
- Adds a little signature to anything you copy, and won't let itself be printed.

## Stopping
Press **Esc twice**, or click **esc esc** in the top corner, to pause at any time.
